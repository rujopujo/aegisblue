AegisBlue MRV: The 4-Step Plain English Workflow

A simple, easy-to-understand breakdown of what each member builds and how it connects together.

💡 The Whole Project in 4 Steps

🗺️
Step 1: Map
User draws boundary over mangrove forest
🛰️
Step 2: Satellite
Calculates NDVI & tons of CO2 stored
⛓️
Step 3: Web3
Mints CO2 tons as tokens on Polygon
📊
Step 4: Market
Companies buy, burn & download ESG PDF

🧑‍💻 Member 1: Map & GIS Lead

Pillar 1

You build the registration map where users draw a polygon box around a coastal mangrove forest.

Your Files: src/components/Pillar1_Registration.tsx & src/services/spatialValidator.ts
What You Pass to Member 2: GPS Coordinates & Total Hectares
📺 React Leaflet Maps Tutorial → 📺 Drawing Polygons on Maps →

🛰️ Member 2: Satellite MRV & Science Lead

Pillar 2

You take the coordinates from Member 1 and calculate NDVI greenness and total CO2 storage.

Your Files: src/components/Pillar2_Auditing.tsx & src/services/satelliteAuditor.ts
What You Pass to Member 3: Total CO2 Tons (e.g. 124,716 tons) & Audit Fingerprint
📺 What is NDVI & Satellite Remote Sensing → 📺 Sentinel-2 Satellite Bands →

⛓️ Member 3: Web3 & Blockchain Lead

Pillar 3

You mint the verified CO2 tons as digital tokens on Polygon blockchain and store audit proof on IPFS.

Your Files: src/components/Pillar3_Tokenization.tsx & src/services/web3Registry.ts
What You Pass to Member 4: Minted Token ID & PolygonScan Transaction Hash
📺 Carbon Credit Smart Contracts → 📺 Connecting MetaMask to React →

📊 Member 4: Marketplace & Dashboard Lead

Pillar 4

You build the marketplace where companies buy and retire tokens and download an official ESG PDF certificate.

Your Files: src/components/Pillar4_Marketplace.tsx & src/services/certificateGenerator.ts
What You Deliver: Storefront, ESG Analytics & Verifiable PDF Certificate
📺 PDF Generation in React (jsPDF) → 📺 Dashboard UI in Tailwind CSS →
